🗜️ About CompactPic

CompactPic is a free online image compressor. Shrink JPEG, PNG and WebP photos, convert between formats, resize in bulk, and download everything as a ZIP — with a before/after slider so you can see exactly what changed. 100% client-side: your photos never leave your browser, no signup, no limits, no watermark.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Compression happens entirely in your browser using canvas. You can disconnect from the internet after loading the page and keep working.

Is there a file limit?

No. Unlimited files, unlimited size, free forever. Your browser's memory is the only limit.

Which formats are supported?

Input: JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF (GIFs are flattened to their first frame). Output: JPEG, PNG or WebP — or keep the original format.

What quality should I use?

80% is a great default — usually 60–80% smaller with no visible difference. Product photos: 75–85%. Thumbnails: 60–70%.

Does it work offline?

Yes. CompactPic is an installable PWA — after your first visit it keeps working without internet.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Use the 🐞 Report bug and 💡 Request feature links in the footer — they open a GitHub issue where you can describe what happened. A simple contact form is coming soon for non-technical users.
